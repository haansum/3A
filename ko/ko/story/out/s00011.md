Yara Osei told the cameras she had nothing against Wren. Then she asked which ankle, and did four bars of the wedding dance from the video, the one filmed nine days after the injury that cancelled their first date. The photographers loved it. The Notary, stamping things.

I held Wren's coin, the one she turns over before every walk. Below us a bus braked on the ramp, long and tired.

Wren said, "You know I couldn't stand on it." Not for the cameras. Just said it, and her chin went.

Nobody laughed after that. Yara kept dancing two beats too long, alone, wind peeling the backdrop off its frame behind her.

Saturday she'll be right about the fight. I put the coin in Wren's hand anyway and closed her fingers on it.
