Nakoma Brennan taps the cage-door hinge three times with two fingers.

**I.**

*(The wrong walkout song is still playing. Naledi Solheim comes up the stairs in her hoodie.)*

NAKOMA: Come in. Mind the lip. Want the blue corner? It's quieter.

NALEDI: That's not my song.

NAKOMA: Nobody's stopped it. Lead early tonight, Larkhollow. They pay for the first exchange.

NALEDI: I don't lead.

**II.**

*(The wrong song. The hoodie. The stairs.)*

NAKOMA: Come in. Mind the lip. Want the blue corner? It's quieter.

NALEDI: That's not my song.

NAKOMA: Nobody's stopped it. Lead early tonight, Larkhollow. They pay for the first exchange.

NALEDI: *(runs her zipper up to the chin, then down)* I don't lead. You told me not to. I was fourteen. You wrote it in green pen.

NAKOMA: *(taps the hinge three times)* Then wait, like at Ferrick's. Ninth of March, round three, clock at 2:14. Twice on my forearm.

NALEDI: There were four people on that apron.

NAKOMA: All looking at the door, they said.

NALEDI: It's a good door.

**III.**

*(The wrong song. The hoodie. The stairs. The mesh at shoulder height, and her hand on it.)*

NAKOMA: Come in. Mind the lip. Want the blue corner? It's quieter.

NALEDI: That's not my song.

NAKOMA: Nobody's stopped it. Lead early tonight, Larkhollow. They pay for the first exchange.

NALEDI: I don't lead. You told me not to. Green pen.

NAKOMA: They'll get your name wrong on the broadcast. Don't worry. I'll spell it right on the list of people I beat.

*(Naledi's zipper stops at her chin.)*

NALEDI: You never spelled it right. Thirty-one letters, Comma. Every one says Nalede. I kept them. I'm not going to be the answer to who you beat at Ferrick's.

NAKOMA: It was a good letter, the first one.

NALEDI: It ends in an I. You were seventeen. You knew how to read.

*(Nakoma's hand goes to the hinge and stays there. She doesn't tap.)*

NALEDI: I'll be up before they find my song.

*(The door swings. The latch catches.)*

NAKOMA: Naledi.
